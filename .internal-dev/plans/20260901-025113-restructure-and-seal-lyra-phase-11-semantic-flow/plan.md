# Restructure and seal Lyra phase-11 semantic flow

## Feature

Replace the duplicated phase-11 aggregate, callable, capture, branch, nil-context, and eager-initialization walkers with a shared semantic-flow algebra, parameterized callable summaries, one canonical typed flow analysis, and independently gated immutable graph sealing—without entering phase 12.

## Required Behavior

- Preserve all currently implemented phase-1 through phase-10 behavior and the current phase-11 baseline of 117 passing compiler tests before adding new focused coverage.
- Migrate the compiler test suite to explicit JUnit 5 discovery in a no-semantic-change parity gate, require compiler-module test discovery, preserve all 117 existing test methods, and remove only redundant test-suite launcher mains while retaining genuine process/fixture mains.
- Represent projection paths with typed tuple-member and array-element steps; exact selectors remain sibling-precise, while unknown array indices are wildcard selectors that overlap compatible array elements but never tuple members.
- Represent every identity-bearing array with a canonical abstract allocation/origin identity, including fresh local allocation sites and cross-module origins. Tuples have no identity but preserve routed identities of contained arrays.
- Protect every aggregate identity that crosses a module boundary from mutation in the importing module, even after aliases, containment, narrowing, coalescing, local @mut rebinding, identity-preserving calls, higher-order calls, captures, or projections.
- Keep binding-local mutation permission separate from aggregate identity ownership. Preserve legal mutation of local arrays through an @mut root, including arrays contained below tuple members, while tuple fields remain non-assignable.
- Define one binding/cell flow state with immutable snapshots, deterministic alternatives, strong whole-binding replacement, exact route-local strong replacement, unknown-index weak replacement, and conservative may-state joins for branches and coalescing.
- Preserve strict eager left-to-right and source-position semantics: earlier calls cannot see later assignments, lambda creation does not execute its body, known calls apply captured shared-cell effects, and call alternatives execute from one caller baseline before joining.
- Precompute one ordered, parameterized symbolic transfer summary per lambda. Summaries must model parameter and capture placeholders, return formulas, fresh allocation sites, projections, branch alternatives, captured-cell writes, higher-order parameter invocation, direct/namespace/callable calls, and eager module effects.
- Solve mutually recursive callable-summary equations to a deterministic least fixed point per callable SCC over the finite abstract domain. Function-only recursion remains legal and analysis-terminating; recursive summaries that expose eager value dependencies still contribute those dependencies.
- Treat a dynamic/unknown selector or callable as the finite union of every compatible reachable alternative in the closed whole graph. A well-typed call with no recoverable callable fact is an internal semantic-artifact failure, not silently effect-free and not a new source-language diagnostic.
- Produce one compact immutable SemanticFlowFacts artifact containing semantic events and summaries at relevant declarations, calls, mutations, captures, and eager-effect witnesses; do not publish complete before/after state for every expression and do not retain final-state-only facts.
- Attach canonical SemanticFlowFacts to the final TypedSemanticGraph and expose them read-only for later compiler phases while documenting them as internal, non-serialized compiler representation rather than stable Java product API.
- Separate typed flow/effect evaluation from initialization graph construction. The planner consumes canonical eager-effect facts, emits deterministic dependencies/witnesses/order/cycles, and keeps function-signature SCC linkage distinct from eager value-cycle analysis.
- Replace scalar duplicated nil-context helpers with one pure structural context-plan law covering final block expressions, arrays, tuples, full conditionals, coalescing child positions, array/equality peers, and exact nested nil positions. TypeChecker selects peer/expected bases; TypedSemanticProvenance independently reconstructs source-derived bases before applying the same law.
- Then-only conditionals remain Unit-valued while their branch is independently typed and evaluated for effects. Bare #NIL or structurally nil-only alternatives without an expected/peer base remain LYC-TYPE failures with no partial graph.
- Preserve diagnostic code, phase, exact primary span, exact related-span witnesses, deterministic related-span ordering, and no partial failed-phase artifact. Diagnostic prose and labels may be clarified without freezing rendered text.
- Narrow ad-hoc TypedSemanticGraph constructors and withInitializationPlan to one package-owned sealing path. Final publication validates complete topology/provenance, flow-fact coverage/invariants, and initialization-plan derivation without rerunning the semantic evaluator.
- Delete each consumer's replaced private route/call/provenance walkers at its incremental cutover; do not leave old and new interpreters running in parallel through final sealing.
- Require focused positive and negative tests, deterministic repetition, full diagnostic shape, no implementation exceptions, no partial artifacts, and independent read-only validation at every hard gate before progression.

- Preserve the accepted language-core and backend-runtime contracts: immutable phase artifacts, fail-fast structured diagnostics, exact identities/spans/links, strict eager left-to-right behavior, source-ordered declarations, binding-local @mut, shared mutable captures, and separate legal function SCCs versus illegal eager value cycles.
- Keep the flow representation internal and JVM-independent. It is not serialized IR, runtime control flow, a general effect system, or a public plug-in API.
- Do not introduce a blanket imported-taint bit, integer-only untyped selector paths, historical candidate accumulation, full relational branch proving, unbounded path expansion, or recursion guards that silently discard effects.

- Do not add a property-testing dependency solely for route laws; use deterministic exhaustive bounded matrices and focused fixtures.
- Keep existing Domain11SemanticTest behavior as integration smoke coverage while adding focused suites; do not rely on cached target reports or manual main methods as validation.

## Assumptions

- The current worktree baseline is intentional and compiles under the available Java 25 profile; inspection established `mvn -pl lyra-compiler clean test` and `mvn clean verify` both pass 117 tests, with `git diff --check` clean.
- The current language slice is a closed whole-reachable graph with no arbitrary Java callbacks or dynamic interop, so callable uncertainty can be represented as a finite union of compatible source lambdas and intrinsic summaries.
- Allocation-site abstract identities are sufficient for compile-time ownership/alias/eager-effect analysis; runtime object identity remains a later IR/backend concern.
- Existing declaration, reference, lambda, capture, module IDs and immutable typed-expression objects provide sufficient semantic site identity; no new cross-build expression ID or serialized flow format is required unless implementation evidence proves otherwise and is escalated.
- JUnit 5 migration may alter test harness files and compiler POM configuration but must not change source-language semantics or reduce baseline coverage.
- Publicly readable SemanticFlowFacts types are internal compiler model exposed only for cross-package compiler consumption; they carry no external compatibility promise.
- Intrinsic functions used during initialization receive explicit finite summaries; a missing summary/callable at a well-typed call indicates an internal compiler defect rather than an expected source error.

## Settled Decisions

- Use a shared internal flow kernel plus staged gates, not continuing local map/set patches and not replacing the semantic pipeline with one monolithic interpreter.
- Attach canonical compact flow facts to TypedSemanticGraph; resolver uses the same laws for early diagnostics but the typed analysis is the canonical phase-12 handoff.
- Migrate all compiler tests to explicit JUnit 5 in a separate parity gate and remove redundant manual suite mains after all 117 tests are discovered.
- Perform incremental cutover per owner and delete replaced walkers at each exit rather than shadow-running both interpreters through 11E.
- Narrow graph construction to one package-owned sealer and expose immutable facts read-only; do not preserve accidental public construction compatibility.
- Retain semantic events and summaries at relevant sites rather than full per-expression state snapshots or final-state-only summaries.
- Use one parameterized ordered symbolic summary per lambda, substitute exact caller facts at calls, and solve recursive callable SCC equations to a deterministic least fixed point.
- Preserve diagnostic code, phase, primary span, and related spans while allowing improved wording.
- Split the route-kernel and evaluator cutovers into hard subgates within the accepted 11A–11E sequence.
- Require independent validation at every hard gate and full reactor verification at each 11A–11E boundary.
- Use exact known routes and wildcard unknown array selectors; exact assignment is strong, unknown-index assignment is weak, whole-binding replacement is strong, and branch/coalesce state is a may-state join.
- All imported aggregate identities remain protected, including imported immutable bindings copied into local @mut bindings.
- Function-only recursion, including invocation during initialization without eager value reads, is not an eager initialization cycle; symbolic summary closure must terminate and still expose any transitive eager reads.
- Use a structural context plan keyed by semantic child positions so coalescing and nested conditionals do not incorrectly nil-lift an entire result.

- Sealing tests prove SemanticFlowFacts and all nested collections are immutable; every relevant declaration/lambda/call/mutation/capture/effect site is covered; missing/foreign/wrong route, summary, scope, span, conversion, dependency, or schedule data is rejected; and valid repeated compilation produces equal facts and graph output.

- Relaxing the rule that every imported aggregate identity is externally read-only or adding ownership-transfer/copy syntax.
